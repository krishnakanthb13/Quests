The Physics of Survival

For most of history, "I Did It" was a claim of physical endurance. To get water was to survive a journey.

Technology has systematically removed the caloric cost of survival. We moved from The Survivalist (carrying 20kg) to The Modernist (paying a bill). The pride of physical burden has been replaced by the expectation of infrastructure.

  • Muscle Era Pure biological output. High caloric burn.
  • Tool Era Mechanical leverage. Efficiency focus.
  • AI/Infra Era Zero marginal effort. System abstraction.
